# Pricing

Register once, no card, and the voice loop is yours forever.

## Free, permanently

- Voice coding with any supported agent
- The on-device neural voice and speech recognition
- Multi-project routing and per-project voices
- Screenshots, hotkeys, silent mode, approve-before-send
- The [local meeting recorder](../guides/record-a-meeting.md), unlimited in length, count, and retention

There are no minutes to buy for any of the above, because the work happens on your machine and costs the vendor nothing to run.

## The one paid feature

[Sending your agent into a video meeting](../guides/send-your-agent.md) is billed per minute through AgentCall, with free hours included. That call runs in the cloud, which is why it is the exception.

## Registration

A short trial runs first. After it, continued use asks for a free sign-in through AgentCall. No card is involved unless you go over the included AgentCall hours.
