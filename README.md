# SKI documentation

SKI gives your coding agent a voice. You speak, your agent works, and it answers out loud in a natural voice generated on your own machine. Speech recognition and speech synthesis both run on-device, so nothing you say is uploaded anywhere.

It works with Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Gemini CLI, Windsurf and OpenClaw, on macOS and Windows, and it is free for life.

## What SKI is not

SKI is not a dictation tool. Dictation stops at text-in: you speak, words appear, and you are back to reading a screen. SKI closes the loop. Your agent's reply is synthesized locally and spoken aloud, and full-duplex echo cancellation means you can cut in mid-sentence and still be heard.

SKI also does not type into your editor or terminal. It talks to your agent through a small skill and plain files inside your own project, which is why it works with any terminal, editor, or IDE you already use.
