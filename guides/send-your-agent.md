# Send your agent to a meeting

Cannot make the call? Send the agent. It joins as a real participant with its own tile and the name you give it, while the transcript writes itself back home.

<!-- widget:stepper -->

### Open the meeting menu

Hover the SKI widget, open the meeting menu, and choose **Send agent to a meeting**.

### Paste the meeting link

Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams all work. Name the bot whatever you like. You can also create a fresh meeting straight from the widget.

### Pick a mode

**Agent** speaks, answers questions, and can summarize the discussion live in the call. **Transcript only** is a silent notetaker for when you just need the record.

### Join and stay heads-down

Your agent walks in. In agent mode it sees the shared screen, can share its own, and reads and sends meeting chat. Back home, the transcript writes itself with every speaker tagged by name.

### Collect the notes

When the call ends, the summary, decisions, and action items are already written by your agent from the diarized transcript, waiting in your Transcripts window.

<!-- /widget -->

## Everyone can see it

The agent joins as a visible participant with its own tile. There is no hidden recording. Any consent the meeting requires is still your responsibility.
