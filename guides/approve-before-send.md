# Approve before send

By default, what you say goes straight to the agent. Approve-before-send puts a checkpoint in between.

## What changes

Every transcript lands in an editable bubble on the widget. You can fix a misheard word, cut a sentence you did not mean, or drop the whole thing. Nothing reaches the agent until you hit the confirm control.

## When it earns its place

- **Noisy rooms**, where recognition picks up more than you intended.
- **Destructive requests**, where "delete the migration" and "delete the migrations" are different afternoons.
- **Shared spaces**, where a colleague's sentence might otherwise land in your session.
- **Learning the tool**, when you want to see what the agent is actually receiving before you trust it.

## Turning it on

It is an optional review mode in preferences. Turn it off again when the friction stops being worth it, which for most people is after the first week.
